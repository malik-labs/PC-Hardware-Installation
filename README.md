<h2>Environments and Technologies Used</h2>

- Enterprise network laser printer, network switch, Ethernet
- Desktop PC hardware: SATA data and power connectors, motherboard, PCIe x16 slot

---

## 1. Enterprise network printer

▶️ [Watch the video](https://youtube.com/shorts/Cjk1xGl8fe0)

Physical setup of an enterprise network laser printer.

1. **Power:** connect the power cable and switch the printer on.
2. **Network:** plug an **Ethernet** cable from the printer into a **network switch**, so it sits directly on the network and users can print to it.
3. **Toner:** fit the toner cartridge. The status lights change from **red to green** once it is seated correctly.

**What I learned:** the printer **beeps** if the toner is missing or not seated properly. On a helpdesk that is one of the first things to check when someone reports a printer error.

---

## 2. SATA SSD (1 TB)

▶️ [Watch the video](https://youtube.com/shorts/qatlpxOG1_Q)

Fitted in an open PC that already had parts installed.

1. **What an SSD does:** it stores the operating system, programs and files, and is much faster than a traditional hard drive because it has no moving parts.
2. **Connect the data cable:** a **SATA data cable** runs from the SSD to a SATA port on the motherboard.
3. **Connect the power cable:** a **SATA power cable** from the power supply plugs into the SSD.

**What I learned:** a SATA drive needs two separate cables, one for data and one for power, and both connectors only fit one way round.

---

## 3. Graphics card (NVIDIA GTX 1650)

▶️ [Watch the video](https://youtube.com/shorts/bxzrwdbw2YM)

Shown on a bare motherboard, so the slot and the card are easy to see.

1. **What a GPU does:** it handles the graphics for the PC, which gives smoother performance in visual work and games.
2. **Find the slot:** the card goes in the long **PCIe x16** slot on the motherboard.
3. **Seat the card:** line the card's connector up with the slot and press it down evenly until it clicks into place.

**What I learned:** a graphics card has its own fan and heatsink to cool itself, because it runs hot under load.
